Circle

A relationship maintenance app for people who care but forget.

Duration: 3 weeks

OVERVIEW

Circle is a mobile app concept designed for people who move to new cities for work and gradually lose touch with their close ones—not because they stop caring, but because life gets in the way. The app tracks how long it has been since you last connected with the people you’ve chosen to keep close. It gives people a visual, emotionally honest signal of where each relationship stands, and makes the act of reaching out as frictionless as one tap.

PROBLEM

We don't drift from the people we love. We just run out of time — and then out of excuses.

When you move cities for work, you don't stop loving your people. You just stop having the time. A week passes without calling Dad. Then two. Then it’s been a month and the thought of calling after so long feels awkward. So you don’t. The gap grows. The relationship quietly drifts. This isn’t a character flaw. It’s a design problem.

RESEARCH

Insight 01

Friction, not intention, is the problem. The solution needed to collapse the distance between “I should call them” and actually calling.

Insight 02

Guilt is the enemy of engagement. Circle needed to feel like a warm nudge, never a judgment.

The research started with a behavioural observation: people don’t fail to stay in touch because they don’t care. They fail because there’s no trigger at the right moment, and because the activation energy of reaching out after a long gap feels disproportionately high.

PS

Name: Priya Sharma

Age: 26

Profession: Working Professional

Goals: Stay connected with family and close friends. Make relationship maintenance feel effortless. Instantly know who needs attention.

Frustrations: Often realizes she’s lost touch too late. Longer gaps make reconnecting feel harder.

SOLUTION

A relationship health tracker that makes staying in touch feel effortless — not obligatory.

Circle visualises relationship health as a draining battery—green when you’re consistent, yellow when it’s been a while, red when you’ve waited too long, and a broken heart when the gap has gone on too long. The app never lectures. It nudges. Warmly. Specifically. And when you’re ready to reach out, it takes one tap.

SCREENS & DESIGN DECISIONS

Login / Signup

Only Name and Phone number are required—no unnecessary fields. Reducing entry friction improves onboarding completion before users experience the app’s core value.

Homepage

Instead of a blank screen, the empty state leads with a warm line and a single clear CTA—making it motivating, not intimidating.

Priority Queue Sort

Auto-prioritising contacts removes decision-making. Users instantly see who needs attention first, reducing cognitive load.

✓ Button on Card

The most common action—logging a connection—is accessible in one tap, reducing the distance between intent and action.

Connect Confirmation

Two sheets separate two cognitive tasks: a quick yes/no followed by a reflective memory moment. The memory field becomes a long-term relationship diary.

Relationship Profile

The chronological memory log grows quietly in the background, giving users a diary of important relationships without asking them to write one.

Custom Frequency

The custom option is visually distinct from presets. Progressive disclosure keeps the main screen clean while supporting edge cases.

Notifications

Personal, specific notifications create recognition that triggers action. “Connect Now” lives inside the card so users don’t need to navigate back.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Colour palette

Deep Rose #A83850 carries warmth, intimacy and quiet urgency. Warm Gold #D2A13F brings optimism and maps to reconnection.

Typography

Pathway Extreme is used for the editorial case-study voice, paired with Nunito for product UI. The system prioritises clear hierarchy, compact labels and readable body copy.
