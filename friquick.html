FriQuick

A mobile cooking assistant that lets busy Indian users have a hands-free cooking session, without the scroll-and-decide fatigue of recipe blogs.

Duration: 6 weeks

PROBLEM

  • Recipe content is optimised for SEO, not for someone standing in a kitchen deciding what to eat.
  • Dietary needs like Jain, vegan, keto and low-carb are bolted on as filters instead of shaping the feed from the start.
  • Once cooking starts, hands are wet or busy—but the phone still needs constant tapping.
  • Saved recipes pile up with no structure, making old recipes hard to find.

DESIGN GOALS

  • Cut the time between opening the app and picking a dish.
  • Make cooking genuinely hands-free, not just “large text.”
  • Make saving and re-finding recipes a non-event, not a task.

RESEARCH

Started with a competitive teardown of mainstream recipe apps along with informal conversations with six target users.

Insight 01

Dietary filtering is almost always a post-search filter, so users repeat the same filter every session.

Insight 02

Voice, where present, is limited to search. None guide the actual cooking process hands-free.

AV

Name: Aryan Verma

Age: 29

Profession: Working Professional

Goals: Cooks 4–5 times a week and wants a proper meal in under 30 minutes after a 10-hour workday.

Frustrations: Recipe videos feel slow and have lots of ad breaks. Inspiration isn’t the problem—knowing what to do quickly is.

SOLUTION

  • Personalise before browsing — capture diet upfront so irrelevant recipes never enter the feed.
  • Hands-free cooking — voice-guided mode built around three taught commands, not a general assistant.
  • Effortless organising — saving and structuring a recipe happen in a single tap.

WIREFRAMES

SCREENS & DESIGN DECISIONS

Preferences

Capture diet preferences before recommending recipes. Every screen after this is already relevant—zero repeated filtering.

Homepage

Open-ended feeds make “what should I cook?” harder. Cuisine icons plus Recommended and Trending rows cut discovery to two visual decisions.

Recipe cards

Keep only the two chips that matter most—diet and cook time. The mic icon can jump directly into voice assist when the user wants to start cooking.

Recipe detail

Long descriptions delay commitment. Scannable meta-tags and a nutrition strip create a faster path from detail to cook.

Voice-assist onboarding

Teach exactly three commands—start, pause and stop—in three bottom sheets before first use. This removes the biggest barrier to hands-free cooking.

Voice-assist screen

Cooking requires messy or wet hands. A high-contrast screen with a live waveform and just two controls keeps guidance focused.

My Cookbook

Bookmarked and Recently Viewed create two practical paths for finding a recipe again, even when users forget to bookmark it.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Colour palette

Primary #1FAA3F signals freshness and action. Secondary #FEDD4D creates appetite, warmth and momentum.

Typography

Nunito is the product typeface, supported by Pathway Extreme for case-study headings. The UI scale follows a Material 3-inspired hierarchy.
