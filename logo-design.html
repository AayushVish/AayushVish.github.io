Pratishruti 2025 — Logo Design

PROJECT BRIEF

Pratishruti is RBU’s annual cultural fest. For its 25th edition, I was responsible for designing the official logo, representing the theme “Arcadia of Realms.” The challenge was to visualise a surreal coexistence of multiple realities and inner emotional paradoxes.

ABOUT THE THEME

This year’s theme, Arcadia of Realms, explored the chaos and clarity of colliding realities, internal conflicts and the journey toward self-realization—a concept termed “Mystopia.” The visual had to communicate balance: a space where illusion and truth coexist.

DESIGN RATIONALE

Dinosaur / Monster represents inner chaos and primal emotion—wild, but part of us—and the dinosaur era as Reality 1. Superman Cape represents the superhero universe as Reality 2. The fusion of fonts symbolises the coexistence of different realities and cultures. Vivid purples, reds and cool blues suggest emotional contrasts.

TOOLS USED

Pen & Paper for initial sketches · Figma for digital exploration and finalisation.

ITERATIONS

The process moved from loose visual exploration toward a single mark that could hold the contradictions of the theme while remaining legible across event media.
